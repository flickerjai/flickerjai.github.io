Hi, I’m Jailine González.

I study how communication shapes relationships among people, technologies, and institutions—and how those relationships transform knowledge and authority.

I am a journalist at La Nación in Costa Rica and a research assistant at the University of Costa Rica’s Communication Research Center. My work brings together qualitative research, computational methods, and firsthand experience studying and implementing AI in newsrooms.

About

Across journalism, research, and computing

I studied Computer Science and Software Engineering at the Costa Rica Institute of Technology before earning a BA in Communication Studies, with honors, at the University of Costa Rica. Rather than separate paths, these fields shape how I study people and computational systems together.

Since 2024, I have researched how people domesticate generative AI in everyday life, including how they dispute, reformulate, and provisionally stabilize factual claims through conversations with ChatGPT. I presented this work as first author at AoIR 2026, and a related manuscript is under review at AI & Society. I also completed an ELAP-funded research placement at York University.

At La Nación, I work in data journalism and AI implementation. I have led UX research on its AI-powered news assistant and now lead a mixed-methods study of young adults’ relationships with news. In 2026, I was also selected for the LSE Polis JournalismAI Skills Lab. These experiences continually inform the questions I bring to academic research.

Research interests

Questions that guide my work

Human–AI relationships

How people incorporate generative AI into everyday practices and negotiate its roles as a tool and social actor.

Knowledge and facts

How factual claims are disputed, reformulated, and stabilized, and how people negotiate epistemic authority.

Media and institutions

How platforms and computational infrastructures shape news consumption, visibility, credibility, and institutional authority.

Selected research

Research projects

Selected academic and applied research. For publications, conference presentations, and additional experience, see my CV.

Making Knowledge with ChatGPT

First-author research on how factual claims are disputed, reformulated, and stabilized in human–AI interactions. Presented at AoIR 2026; a related manuscript is under review at AI & Society.

Domesticating Artificial Intelligence in Costa Rica

Ongoing collaborative research at the University of Costa Rica using interviews, participatory workshops, qualitative coding, and inductive analysis to examine how people integrate AI into everyday practices.

Young Audiences and News

Mixed-methods audience research with readers and non-readers ages 18–24, examining news habits, trust, subscriptions, and the design of an AI-powered news product.

UX Research on an AI News Assistant

Led user interviews, usability testing, and analysis of human–AI conversations for Pregúntele a LN, La Nación’s retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) news assistant.
